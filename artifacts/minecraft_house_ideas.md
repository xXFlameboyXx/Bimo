# Minecraft House Design Ideas & Build Guide

## 1. Cozy Woodland Cottage
- **Primary Materials:** Oak Logs, Dark Oak Planks, Spruce Stairs, Cobblestone.
- **Key Features:** Steep pitched roof, warm lantern lighting, flower boxes outside windows, chimney with campfire smoke.
- **Interior Layout:** Crafting nook on the main floor, cozy bed and storage loft upstairs, small basement cellar for potions and enchantment table.

## 2. Modern Cliffside Villa
- **Primary Materials:** White Concrete, Quartz Stairs, Tinted Glass, Smooth Stone, Spruce Accents.
- **Key Features:** Flat cantilevered balconies jutting out from a mountain cliff, floor-to-ceiling glass windows, outdoor infinity pool overlooking a valley.
- **Interior Layout:** Open-concept living space, hidden redstone lighting, subterranean branch mine entry inside the garage.

## 3. Medieval Stone Keep
- **Primary Materials:** Stone Bricks, Mossy Stone Bricks, Deepslate Tile, Spruce Wood, Iron Bars.
- **Key Features:** Guard tower with archer slits, fortified entrance gate, courtyard gardens, banners and wall torches.
- **Interior Layout:** Great hall with long dining table, armor stand display room, secure vault for diamond chests.

## 4. Japanese Style Pagoda Sanctuary
- **Primary Materials:** Dark Oak Wood, Crimson Planks, Red Terracotta, Prismarine Tiles, Paper Walls (White Stained Glass).
- **Key Features:** Multi-tiered curved roofs, cherry blossom surroundings, bamboo garden, bridge over koi pond.
- **Interior Layout:** Tatami mat flooring using carpets, central brewing area, quiet enchanting shrine.

## Pro Tips for Exterior Detailing
1. **Depth:** Avoid flat walls by pushing pillars out one block forward.
2. **Palette:** Stick to 3 or 4 main complementary blocks for a cohesive theme.
3. **Lighting:** Hide light sources under carpets or leaves for clean ambient glow.
